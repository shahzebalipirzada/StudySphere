# 🎓 StudySphere

> Your Personal AI-Powered Learning Operating System

StudySphere is a modern, AI-powered study hub designed to help students learn smarter—not harder. Instead of juggling multiple apps for notes, AI assistance, PDFs, flashcards, quizzes, and planning, StudySphere brings everything together into one intelligent, personalized workspace.

Unlike traditional learning platforms, StudySphere is built around **one student**. The AI learns from your study habits, remembers your progress, and adapts its recommendations to help you improve over time.
